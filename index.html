Every figure is computed from global_fuel_prices_Dataset.csv: 27,468 weekly records, 6 Jan 2020 to 6 Apr 2026. Country averages are means of all weeks. Diesel volatility here is the coefficient of variation (standard deviation divided by mean of weekly diesel price) within each country and year, averaged across a region's countries, so it will not match the volatility calculation in the Tableau workbook. The diesel forecast image is the forecast panel from the workbook.
